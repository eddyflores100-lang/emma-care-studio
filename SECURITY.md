# Security Policy

## Reporting

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's Security Advisories feature (if enabled) or through AliceLabs' published business contact channel. Do not post exploit details, personal information or account data in public issues.

Include the affected version, browser/device, reproduction steps and impact. Do not attach private recordings or personal data.

## Privacy and safety

The game may request microphone permission for browser speech recognition and may use browser-provided speech services. Explain this behavior clearly in user-facing changes. Do not add undisclosed recording, tracking or transmission of voice data. Treat imported/exported world files as user data and validate them before use.
